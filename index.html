How the production version works

1

GHL inbound-SMS webhook — a new text to your GHL number fires a webhook into n8n carrying the contact, message, and conversation history from GHL custom fields.

2

n8n orchestration — the workflow checks business hours, loads the contact's pipeline stage, and routes: FAQ → knowledge base, booking intent → calendar check, low confidence → human handoff.

3

LLM reply with memory — the agent drafts a reply with full conversation context, constrained by guardrails (never quotes final prices, never books outside real availability).

4

Back through GHL — the reply goes out as SMS from your number; the transcript, intent tags, and booked appointment land on the contact record and pipeline automatically.

5

Human handoff — on frustration signals, complex requests, or explicit "call me", the agent warm-transfers with a summary to the assigned rep via GHL internal notification.
